## Multiturn plural comparison context

The pre-fix V2.1 state could lose both projects after a prior comparison: the independent fixture's “When were both projects tested?” selected an unrelated third project, and excerpt mode returned that one passage. The current shared resolver carries explicitly plural project references, keeps singular “it” ambiguous, and requires the topic anchors to occur in the body of a common clause for each project. Hosted excerpt mode returns one cited passage from each project or insufficient evidence. This is a conservative lexical and structural filter, not proof of semantic entailment. It can reject valid facts split across sentences or expressed with unrecognised synonyms, and still accept unrelated relations within a clause. No retrieval threshold was altered.

The first 17-case confirmation was inspected and promoted to development after exposing Japanese `それぞれ` being mistaken for singular `それ`. A later 14-case same-reviewer confirmation, frozen before its initial run, found 4/4 supported with both sources and 7/7 unsupported abstentions after the fix; its later rerun is regression evidence, not blind. A separately frozen 12-case holdout authored after clause and purpose checks gives 4/4 supported two-sided excerpts, 5/5 unsupported abstentions and 3/3 singular clarifications. Pre-fix V2.1 gave no supported two-sided excerpts in that holdout and would enter generation on 3/5 unsupported questions; its evidence packs sometimes contained the third project. These are very small synthetic, same-reviewer samples; no real model was invoked. All individual questions, source sets, decisions, excerpt text and one-shot timings are in `plural-context-*.json`, `plural-fresh-*.json` and `plural-holdout-*.json`.

An additional promoted diagnostic showed a location for an unrelated conference in the same sentence as a test could qualify as the test location. Clause separation rejects that specific counterexample (`plural-relation-*.json`), but it cannot establish relationships within one clause. The separately documented single-turn `f4` comparison miss remains. The original 100-case aggregates are unchanged in the last rerun, and past independent sets remain diagnostic once inspected.

For the 12-case plural holdout, median one-shot preparation plus excerpt time was 14.31 ms before and 16.75 ms after on this machine. This times both local paths sequentially, includes no provider or HTTP latency, and is too small and uncontrolled to establish performance. No generative-model calls were made; the records distinguish decisions to generate from actual calls.
