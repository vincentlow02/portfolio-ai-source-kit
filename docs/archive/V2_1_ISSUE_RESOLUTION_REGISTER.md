### IA-22 — English participant evidence polarity

- **Classification and scope.** Confirmed P1 relevance and cost defect in `lib/portfolio-chat/b2.ts`, affecting model preparation and Hosted excerpts for explicit participant comparisons.
- **Evidence and reproduction.** The offline case `a participant comparison cannot turn negated interviews into study evidence` reproduces the earlier reservation on a pair of synthetic records that state interviews did not occur. Compare `61c46f2` and `f1806e1` with `scripts/evaluation/direct-participant-polarity-fresh.mts`. The earlier revision would generate on four of five labelled negative cases. The later revision abstains on all five and retains the supported pair.
- **Cause and impact.** A past-tense expression inside reported or denied speech qualified as a study event. Visitors could receive an unrelated study excerpt or start a paid generation path without affirmative evidence.
- **Fix and acceptance.** `57e976b`, `456e2b5` and `757c534` require a limited direct clause pattern and reject selected negative, modal and denial wording. The supported `not only interviewed` case remains eligible. The offline test asserts zero reservation on the original negative cases. The fresh synthetic confirmation shows 1/1 supported and 5/5 abstentions.
- **Regression risk and remaining work.** Indirect statements, unrecognized roles or phrasing can cause false positives or false negatives. Independently adjudicate a broader multilingual corpus before claiming general polarity or relationship validation.
